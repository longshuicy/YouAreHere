## A Song of Ice and Fire

*Shipped as `asoiaf.json` — 592 characters, 2619 ties.*

"Character Interaction Networks for A Song of Ice and Fire" by **Andrew Beveridge and Jie Shan** (<https://mathbeveridge.github.io/>)

- Source: <https://github.com/mathbeveridge/asoiaf>
- Project: <https://networkofthrones.wordpress.com>
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) (`CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`)
- Retrieved: 2026-09-17
- Cite as: A. Beveridge and J. Shan, “Network of Thrones”, Math Horizons 23(4), 2016, pp. 18–22. DOI: [10.4169/mathhorizons.23.4.18](https://doi.org/10.4169/mathhorizons.23.4.18)

Changes made to the original data:

1. Merged the five per-book edge lists into one graph, summing tie weights across books.
2. Derived display names from the dataset's hyphenated identifiers.
3. Applied a hand-maintained identity table covering 28 characters: display names, nicknames for the guess field, and disambiguators.
4. Dropped ties weaker than 1 and characters with fewer than 2 ties, then kept the largest connected component (796 nodes and 2823 ties in, 592 and 2619 out).
5. Added a normalised rank per tie endpoint so thickness reads relative to each character's own ties rather than raw counts.
6. Computed a force-directed layout for the full graph, used by the reveal animation.
