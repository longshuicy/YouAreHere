`pipeline/raw/stormlight/SOURCE.md`; this file covers what differs.

## The cast: the animated series

`Category:Avatar: The Last Airbender characters`. The Legend of Korra, the Netflix
series and comics-only characters are out.

## Segments

The three seasons. A tie takes its season from `{{Cite episode|1|Nxx}}` citations in
the paragraphs behind it, where series `1` is the original show and `N` the book.
About half the ties come from uncited paragraphs and carry no season.

## Other canons

The articles continue past the finale into the comics and The Legend of Korra.
Paragraphs citing only comics, novels, films, games or Korra episodes
(`{{Cite episode|2|…}}`) are skipped.
